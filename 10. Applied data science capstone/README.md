# Applied Data Science Capstone (IBM)

* This capstone is the final course in the IBM Data Science Professional Certificate and Applied Data Science with Python Specialization.

* In this project, I take on the role of a Data Scientist at a startup aiming to compete with SpaceX.

* I applied the full data science methodology:

  * Data collection & wrangling

  * Exploratory data analysis & visualization

  * Model development & evaluation

  * Communicating results to stakeholders (via presentation slide)

* My main task was to provide insights and to build machine learning models for predicting whether the first stage of the Falcon 9 rocket would land successfully.
